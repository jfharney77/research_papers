\subsection{2.1  Agent Evaluation and the Deployment Gap}

Pre-deployment evaluation of AI systems has matured substantially. The emergence of standardized benchmarks [9], automated red-teaming pipelines [5, 11], behavioral testing frameworks such as CheckList [12], and alignment techniques such as RLHF [10] and Constitutional AI [1] have created a well-tooled pre-release lifecycle. Post-deployment monitoring has received comparatively less systematic attention. Pan, Arabzadeh, and Ellis [25] conducted the first large-scale empirical study of production AI agents, finding that most deployments fail or underdeliver and that little is publicly known about post-release management. Sapra et al. [27] address detection via AgentCompass---structured post-deployment debugging---but prescribe no remediation. Rath [24] formalizes agent drift across 12 measurable dimensions, projecting a 42\% task success reduction for unchecked drift via simulation. The APIP addresses what all three leave open: a structured remediation protocol once degradation is identified.
